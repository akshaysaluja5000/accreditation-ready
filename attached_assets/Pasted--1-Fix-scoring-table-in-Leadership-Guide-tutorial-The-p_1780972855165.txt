### 3. Remove “My Risk Assessment” from the staff dashboard sidebar

The “My Risk Assessment — AI · Identify your gaps · get a personalized plan”
card in the staff sidebar needs to be removed entirely.

The Diagnostic Quiz already handles gap identification. Having a second
feature with “Risk” in the name creates confusion and the word “risk”
has the wrong connotation for clinical staff — they associate it with
patient safety risk or liability, not a learning tool.

Before removing: please tell Rajit what this feature actually does
technically. If it is genuinely different from the diagnostic quiz
(for example, if it updates continuously as staff answer questions
rather than being a one-time 25-question test), it should be renamed
rather than removed. If it duplicates the diagnostic, remove it.
